01

Overview

Research internship at the Royal Military Academy in Brussels, within the DREAM project. I work on Physical AI for robotic manipulation, with a focus on reinforcement learning, hierarchical reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer.

The research explores how robots can reason about physical interactions and select actions in cluttered scenes. Physics simulation and counterfactual experiments provide a testbed for learning and evaluating manipulation strategies.

02

Requirements & constraints

An object may support or obstruct another. Geometric contact is not sufficient evidence of a causal dependency: removal experiments are needed.

Scene generation must be reproducible, and initial releases should avoid artificial interpenetration. Physics parameters and their uncertainty need explicit treatment.

The long-term architecture must distinguish observing the scene, deciding on an executable action and executing it. Physical validation remains a separate milestone.

03

My contribution

I develop the simulation and experimental pipeline: procedural scene generation, physics checks, Gymnasium-based environments and reinforcement-learning experiments. My current work also explores hierarchical policies and the steps needed for sim-to-real transfer.

04

Approach & decisions

Generate seeded scenes with several objects and configurable physical properties. Settle each scene in MuJoCo and record its initial conditions so experiments can be repeated.

Use removal experiments to investigate interactions between objects. Compare the scene before and after an intervention rather than inferring physical dependencies from contact alone.

Connect simulation to a Gymnasium-based learning pipeline and investigate parallel rollouts, policy updates and compute utilization.

Explore hierarchical reinforcement learning to connect higher-level action selection with lower-level execution. Evaluate the resulting strategies in simulation before investigating transfer to the physical robot.

Check the simulation through free-fall, inertia, friction and multi-seed diagnostics before interpreting learning results. Robot execution and sim-to-real evaluation are subsequent research milestones.

05

Results & validation

Implemented reproducible scene generation, headless and interactive simulation, saved scene descriptions and physics diagnostics.

Reinforcement-learning experiments and evaluation are ongoing. This page presents the research direction and implemented tools; final policy performance and physical robot results are still work in progress.

06

Limitations & next steps

The internship is ongoing. Reliable action selection requires further evaluation across object configurations and physical parameters.

Simulation results depend on material, friction and contact assumptions. Real-world calibration and robot experiments are needed to evaluate transfer.
