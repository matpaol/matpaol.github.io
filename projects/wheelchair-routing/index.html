01

Pipeline

The pipeline geocodes the endpoints, downloads only the area around them (500 m margin) and caches the graph. Edges are enriched with slope, width, kerb, surface and wheelchair tags. If no route satisfies the constraints, the area grows and the limits relax to 15% slope and 1.0 m width, while absolute exclusions stay in place.

02

Results

On eight Brussels scenarios A* and the MDP planner found routes in all eight, the CSP planner in seven. Interactive maps make each comparison inspectable.
