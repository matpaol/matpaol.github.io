01

Overview

A pneumatic soft gripper designed by our team to handle delicate, irregular objects. Four silicone tentacles deform around the object, integrated with a two-degree-of-freedom arm.

02

Requirements & constraints

The design had to balance compliant bending, wall integrity, fabrication and integration. A 200 g payload was a project requirement, not a verified maximum load.

03

My contribution

I worked on the hyperelastic finite-element analysis of the silicone actuator, designed the casting mold and 3D-printed the tooling. The arm, electronics and integrated system were team work.

04

Approach & decisions

The actuator geometry was investigated in simulation before tooling. Mold design connected the internal chamber geometry to a casting process and a removable core.

05

Results & validation

The team published a build guide and a prototype demonstration. The demo documents the integrated system; it does not by itself quantify agreement between the FEM model and measurements.

06

Limitations & next steps

The team documented shoulder rigidity, custom-belt reliability and the absence of active pump regulation as limitations. Quantitative model validation is a separate question.
