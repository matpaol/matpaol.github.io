01

Overview

The project investigates bearing faults using stator-current signals from the Paderborn dataset.

02

Requirements & constraints

Changes in operating conditions and differences between individual bearings complicate generalization.

03

My contribution

I developed and investigated the diagnosis pipeline as a university project in electrical machines and drives.

04

Approach & decisions

The code implements dataset exploration, preprocessing, autoencoder reconstruction residuals and a one-dimensional CNN. The extended protocol separates entire bearings between training and evaluation.

05

Results & validation

The replication-analysis notebook contains executed comparisons and saved outputs. The extended evaluation notebook contains the protocol and implementation but no saved execution results.

06

Limitations & next steps

Replication results should not be reported as final performance on unseen bearings. Operating-condition shifts, specimen effects and leakage must be evaluated explicitly.
